1. DATA CONTROLLER AND INTRODUCTION (SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS)

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application named Nireb, which may be published under different localized app names in different mobile app stores. Depending on the store and the target market, the application may be published on the Google Play Store, the Apple App Store and other app stores that may be used in the future under localized names such as "Nireb: Learn English", "Nireb: Kelime Kartları", "Nireb: Turkish Arabic" or similar. All of these naming variations refer to the same mobile application within the scope of this Privacy Policy, which will hereinafter be referred to as the "Application".

The data controller of the Application is the independent developer Ahmet Yılmaz. "Injah Labs" may be the developer/brand name used for the Application in some app stores; however, it is not a separate company or legal entity and does not act as the data controller. Since the Application is operated by an individual developer, the legal data controller is the natural person Ahmet Yılmaz. In this Privacy Policy, the term "Application Owner" refers to the data controller, Ahmet Yılmaz.

Official email address for questions about privacy, personal data, user rights, data processing or this Privacy Policy in connection with the Application: injahlabs@gmail.com

The Application is a mobile application that does not require users to create an account and is designed to perform its core functions largely on the device. The Application has no online user account, membership or login, and no centralized database holding users' personal data.

The Application is offered free of charge. It has no in-app purchases, paid memberships or subscriptions.

2. DATA AND METHODS OF COLLECTION

The Application does not use a membership or account system that requires users to enter their personal information. The optional profile name (nickname) created by the user in the Application, progress, game state, language preferences, app settings and various counters and time information used for the operation of the Application are kept in the device's local storage. This information is not transferred to any server or user database operated by the Application Owner.

The core functions of the Application can be used without an internet connection; ads and certain third-party services depend on an internet connection. Operations such as tracking life refill times, technical checks against changes to the device time, daily limits on ad frequency and the interval between interstitial ads are carried out within the Application's local operating mechanisms, and the related records are kept only on the device. As part of the check against time changes, when the device is connected to the internet, the Application may send a short connection request to a server operated by Google solely to obtain the current time. No progress, nickname or other application data is sent with this request; however, as with any internet connection, the device's IP address may be visible to the relevant server during the connection.

Daily reminder notifications are optional; they are scheduled on the device, shown only with the user's permission, and no data is sent for them. Words are read aloud using the device's own text-to-speech (TTS) feature, which is subject to the rules of the device's operating system or the selected voice engine. The options to rate and share the Application open the app store or the device's share menu; the content of these actions is not visible to the Application Owner.

Google AdMob and Google Firebase services are used for the Application's advertising and for evaluating its technical operation. These services may automatically process certain technical data related to the device and its use through the relevant SDKs and service connections used in the Application.

The Application does not ask the user for their name, surname, phone number, email address, password, contacts, photos or similar personal content. If the user voluntarily sends an email to our contact address, the email address and the information in the message may be processed to handle the communication and respond to the request.

For technical error tracking and development of the Application, technical data such as device, application and operating system information, error/crash information and randomly generated identifiers specific to the app installation may be processed for the operation of the third-party services used. To understand the Application's user base and how it is used at a general level, aggregate statistics such as approximate user numbers by country, overall download and uninstall statistics and general usage rates of features may be used. These statistics are not used to directly identify a specific user.

Which technical data third-party services process in their own systems, how this data is used and stored, and the choices users have regarding it are subject to the current policies and settings of the respective service providers. For details:

3. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING AND LEGAL BASES

3.1. Processing of Data on the Device

The data kept on the device by the Application is used to run the Application's core functions, preserve the user's progress in the Application, operate game features, refill lives after the set time, apply technical checks that protect the integrity of the Application against changes to the device time, and limit ad impressions so that they do not negatively affect the user experience.

3.2. Google AdMob

The ads shown in the Application are provided through the Google AdMob service. The purpose of AdMob is the technical delivery and measurement of the ads that allow the Application to be offered free of charge and, where applicable, the personalization of ads.

Within this service, the device advertising ID (Android AAID / Apple IDFA), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address (country or city level; precise location is not used), device type, operating system information and ad interaction data may be processed in order to deliver ads securely, prevent invalid clicks and measure performance. Whether ads are personalized may be determined according to the user's region, current privacy preferences and Google's rules and settings for the relevant advertising technologies.

If the laws of your country or region require it, a consent screen asking for your advertising and data preferences is shown when the Application is first opened, and a "Privacy choices" section is available in the Application's Settings menu so that you can change these preferences later. Whether this screen and section are shown depends solely on the requirements of local law. On Apple devices, the tracking permission provided by the operating system (App Tracking Transparency) is requested separately. If personalized ads are not allowed, ads are shown without personalization or, in some cases, may not be shown at all; this does not affect the use of the Application. Regardless of their location, all users can reset or delete their advertising ID and turn off personalized ads in their device settings.

Detailed information about these processes carried out by Google AdMob can be found in Google's current privacy and advertising policies:
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google AdMob – Personalized and non-personalized ads: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/7676680

3.3. Google Firebase

The relevant Google Firebase services are used to evaluate the technical reliability of the Application, detect errors and understand on which devices and operating system conditions the Application experiences problems.

In addition, usage data is processed to create aggregate statistics that help understand how the Application is generally used. In this context, aggregate measurements such as the approximate distribution of users across countries, the overall level of use of the Application and the usage rates of core features may be evaluated. The purpose of these statistics is not to identify a specific user but to understand general trends for developing the Application.

Within Google Firebase services, device model, operating system version, app version, randomly generated identifiers specific to the app installation and temporary network data (IP) may be processed in order to detect technical errors in the Application and produce approximate country-based usage statistics. If the laws of your location require consent, usage statistics are collected according to the choice you make on the consent screen; if consent is not given, only limited data is sent, or none at all. Uygulama kapsamında gerçekleştirilen veri işleme faaliyetleri, uygulanabilir mevzuatta öngörülen hukuki sebeplere dayanır. Bu kapsamda:

  • Cihaz üzerinde tutulan veriler: Uygulamanın kullanıcıya sunulabilmesi için gereklidir ve yalnızca cihazda işlenir.

  • Yetkili makamların talepleri: Hukuki yükümlülüklerin yerine getirilmesi.

Türkiye'deki kullanıcılar bakımından bu işlemler, 6698 sayılı Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu'nun (KVKK) 5. maddesinde sayılan hukuki sebeplere ve gerekli hallerde açık rızaya dayanır.
